\begin{frame}{Results Summary}

\KeyPoint{CATRA generally improves fairness across asymmetric multi-hop scenarios while keeping total channel utilization reasonably good.}

\vspace{0.35cm}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Long-hop and short-hop flows become substantially fairer.
    \item Central-flow starvation is reduced in asymmetric carrier-sensing topologies.
    \item Short-term fairness improves when one flow starts earlier than another.
    \item TCP and UDP throughputs become closer under CATRA.
    \item Random topology results show a higher Jain Fairness Index.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}
