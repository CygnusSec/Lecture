\begin{frame}{Conclusion}

\KeyPoint{CATRA demonstrates that TCP fairness in multi-hop ad hoc networks should be addressed as a joint MAC-layer and transport-layer problem.}

\vspace{0.35cm}

\begin{itemize}
    \item MAC-layer CW adaptation targets per-station fairness.
    \item TCP rate adaptation targets per-flow fairness.
    \item Cross-layer information helps align channel access with traffic demand.
    \item The approach improves fairness, but introduces fairness--throughput trade-offs.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}
